About Me

Hi, I'm Bruno, a mobile developer who loves turning ideas into fast, well-structured apps.

I graduated in Computer Science, and my final project was a convolutional neural network that recognizes the rooms of a simulated house from images. Today my day-to-day is Flutter: I build apps organized with Clean Architecture and SOLID, state management with BLoC, dependency injection with GetIt, REST integrations with Dio and local persistence with SQLite.

Beyond code, I have a design background, so I care about the details of the interface and the user experience. I'm always looking for new challenges where I can combine technical knowledge and creativity.
